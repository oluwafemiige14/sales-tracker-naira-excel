# Sales Tracker in Naira (Excel)

An Excel tracker that shows revenue, expenses and profit by salesperson, city and product using SUMIF formulas.

## Business problem

A small sales team needs to see who sold what, where, and at what profit, without recalculating by hand.

## Approach

- Recorded each sale by salesperson, city, product, units sold and price
- Calculated revenue, expenses and profit for every row
- Used SUMIF formulas to total revenue and profit by city and by product, and to show the top earner

## Limitations

- A small sample of data
- SUMIF only matches exact entries, so a misspelled city or product name quietly breaks a total

## How I handled them

- Standardised city and product names before summing
- Checked formula totals against the raw entries

## Outcome

One sheet that shows revenue and profit by city and product and the top earner, ready to update as new sales are added.

## Tools

Excel (SUMIF, calculated columns)
